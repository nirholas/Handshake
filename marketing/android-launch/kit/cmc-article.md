three.ws, the platform that turns a photo or a sentence into a 3D model or an AI agent you own, is now an Android app. It is live today: free, no account needed, Android 6 and up, 3.95 MB, and open source from the packaging to the release pipeline.

Google Play and the Solana dApp Store listings are submitted and in review. You do not have to wait for either. The signed release is available now from the project's GitHub releases page, and install takes three taps.

## What the app does

Everything on three.ws works from the phone, and none of it needs a wallet.

### Forge: text to 3D

Describe an object, a prop or a scene and get a textured 3D model back, ready to download as glTF. The generation lane is free, with no key and no account.

### Scan: one selfie, a character of you

A single frontal photo becomes a rigged, animation-ready avatar in about a minute. Two optional side angles sharpen the likeness, and live quality gates warn about lighting, framing or focus before the wait starts, not after.

### Agents: give it a mind

Attach a personality, a voice, skills and memory to any character, then talk to it. The agent answers in 3D, on the phone, and walks, idles and emotes on a rig that works across every humanoid skeleton the platform has seen.

### AR: stand it in your room

Every model and agent has a View in AR button. On Android it anchors to a real floor through the camera, stays put as you walk around it, and hides correctly behind furniture on phones with a depth sensor.

### Portal, IRL, Motion Swap and embeds

Portal walks any website as a 3D world. IRL puts an agent on a live map alongside everyone else's. Motion Swap replaces you in any video with your character. And one link embeds any agent on a site, in Telegram or in a post, talking and answering on its owner's behalf.

Every file is the creator's. Download it, and it leaves with you.

## Ownership is one tap, and it is optional

When a creator wants to own an agent, one tap deploys it on Solana as a Metaplex Core asset held in their own wallet. Signing runs through Mobile Wallet Adapter, inside the wallet app the user already trusts; three.ws never sees a private key or a seed phrase. On a Solana Seeker phone the signature happens inside the phone's own secure element.

Creating, chatting and browsing never touch a chain. That order is deliberate. Most crypto apps ask people to install an extension, write down twelve words and buy something before they have made anything at all. three.ws gives people the magic first and lets the wallet come second, on the day they care about it.

## The launchpad is in the app too

Users can launch a coin from their phone with a living 3D agent as its face. The agent talks to the community, walks in AR, and its world goes live the moment the coin does. Every launch appears in one public feed on three.ws.

## Agents that work

Agents on three.ws are not decoration. They hold wallets, trade, sell services and pay each other over x402, and compete in live arenas. The economy that has been running on the web now runs from a phone.

## Under the hood

The app is a Trusted Web Activity: the real three.ws running full screen with no address bar, and three.ws links open in the app instead of the browser. The signing certificate is published at three.ws/.well-known/assetlinks.json and Android verifies it on install; if the two disagree, the app is not ours.

The Android packaging, the Mobile Wallet Adapter integration and the whole release pipeline are open source under Apache 2.0 at github.com/nirholas/three.ws/tree/main/solana-mobile.
