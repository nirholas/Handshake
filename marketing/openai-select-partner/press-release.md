## three.ws Named an OpenAI Select Partner

**Wilmington, Del. - July 25, 2026** - three.ws, the open-source platform for 3D
AI agents, today announced that it has been named an OpenAI Select Partner
within the OpenAI Partner Network.

The OpenAI Partner Network is a global program for partners to build, sell, and
deliver AI solutions with OpenAI. It brings together partners with deep industry
expertise, delivery capabilities, and customer relationships while equipping them
with resources, enablement, and support to help enterprises adopt OpenAI frontier
models and products and turn them into measurable impact.

As an OpenAI Select Partner, three.ws will continue working with OpenAI to help
organizations build, deploy, and scale AI solutions responsibly and effectively.
three.ws contributes the dimension assistants have been missing: its free 3D
Studio connector gives ChatGPT eleven keyless tools that turn a sentence into an
interactive, textured 3D model rendered inline in the conversation, then rig it,
animate it, and place it in the user's room in augmented reality.

"Assistants have been excellent at text and images for years and have had almost
nothing to say in three dimensions," said [Executive Name], [Title], three.ws.
"A model you can orbit, rig, animate, and stand on your desk is a different kind
of answer than a picture of one. Being named an OpenAI Select Partner is a
mandate to make that response type ordinary, and to keep the standards under it
open so the whole ecosystem can use them."

three.ws supports developers, studios, and enterprises building embodied AI
experiences across gaming, commerce, education, and customer engagement. Its work
includes the three.ws 3D Studio connector for ChatGPT, which is free, keyless,
and operator-funded; a REST Actions surface for custom GPTs on plans without
connector support; device-aware augmented-reality export that converts generated
models to Apple Quick Look USDZ on the fly for iOS and issues Scene Viewer
intents on Android; and Spatial MCP, an open CC0 specification for returning a
live 3D scene as a first-class tool result rather than a bare file URL. The
entire stack, including the viewer, the runtime, and the embeddable web
component, is open source.

Looking ahead, three.ws plans to deepen its OpenAI-related offerings: broader
expression and behaviour range for generated avatars in the inline widget, wider
emission of conformant Spatial MCP artifacts so 3D results stay portable across
clients, and continued investment in the open standards that let any assistant,
not only ChatGPT, answer in three dimensions.

Learn more about three.ws at https://three.ws/openai. Learn more about the OpenAI
Partner Network: https://openai.com/business/partners/

### About three.ws

three.ws is an open-source platform for 3D AI agents. It turns a text prompt into
a rigged, animation-ready 3D character, gives that character an on-chain identity
and a payment rail, and embeds it into any website or assistant with a single
tag. The free 3D generation lane requires no account, no API key, and no payment.
