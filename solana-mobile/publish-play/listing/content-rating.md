# Content rating questionnaire answers

Play Console > App content > Content rating. The questionnaire is issued by IARC and produces
ratings for every region at once. Answer conservatively: a rating that is too low and later
corrected can pull the listing, and there is no upside to under-declaring.

**Category:** Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other.

| Question area | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Violence, realistic or cartoon | No | No combat, no weapons, no depictions of violence |
| Sexuality or nudity | No | Avatar creation is clothed by default; sexual content is prohibited by our terms |
| Profanity or crude humor | No | None in the product surface |
| Controlled substances | No | None |
| Gambling, simulated or real | **No** | This one deserves a real answer rather than a reflex. Users can trade agents and buy skills with USDC, and agents can be deployed as on-chain assets. None of it is a wager: there is no game of chance, no randomized paid draw, no loot box, no prize pool, and no mechanism where a user pays for a chance at an item of unknown value. Play's blockchain policy bans exactly that pattern and we do not implement it |
| User-generated content | **Yes** | Users create avatars and agent personas that other users can see in the marketplace |
| UGC: moderation and reporting | **Yes** | Report and takedown paths exist and are staffed through support@three.ws |
| Users can interact or exchange content | Yes | Marketplace listings, agent chat, embeds |
| Shares user location | No | Never collected |
| Allows purchases | Yes | On-chain purchases settled by the user's own wallet, not Play Billing. See financial-features.md |
| Digital purchases of virtual currency | No | three.ws sells no in-app currency and mints no purchasable token |

**Expected outcome:** Teen in North America, PEGI 12 in Europe, driven by the user-generated
content and interaction answers rather than by anything in the app's own content.

**Target audience:** 18 and over. Select 18+ explicitly. The app is not designed for children,
must not appear in Play's Families programs, and the 18+ answer keeps it out of them.
