three.ws turns a photo or a prompt into 3D models and AI agents you own.

Describe an object and get a textured 3D model back. Take a selfie and get a rigged, animation-ready 3D avatar in about a minute. Give a character a personality and a voice, talk to it, and put it anywhere on the internet with one line of HTML. No 3D software, no modelling skills, no account required to look around.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Make a 3D model from text. Describe a prop, an object or a scene and the free generation lane builds it, textured and ready to download as glTF. No key, no account, no cost.

Make an avatar from a selfie. One frontal photo becomes a rigged avatar; two optional side angles sharpen the likeness. Live quality gates tell you if the lighting, framing or focus will hurt the result before you spend a minute waiting.

Make a character from a description. No photo? Describe the character and get a rigged avatar. Already have a model? Upload a GLB and it is rigged for you.

Give it a mind. Attach a personality, a voice and skills, then hold a conversation with it. The agent answers in 3D, on your phone.

Discover. Browse the whole community's models and agents. Drag to orbit, tap to inspect, open any agent to chat with it.

Own it on-chain, if you want to. Deploy an agent as a Metaplex Core asset on Solana held by your own wallet. This is entirely optional: creating, chatting and browsing never touch a wallet.

Embed anywhere. Every agent has a shareable link and a one-line embed snippet that works in a blog post, a docs site, Notion, or your own product.

See it in your room. Point your camera and place any model or agent in real space through your browser's AR support. Nothing to install.

WALLETS ARE OPTIONAL

You can use three.ws without ever connecting a wallet. If you do connect one, signing runs through Mobile Wallet Adapter, so it happens inside the wallet app you already trust and this app never sees a private key or a seed phrase. three.ws is not a wallet, does not hold your funds, and does not operate an exchange.

OPEN BY DEFAULT

three.ws is open source at github.com/nirholas/three.ws. Agents are published with an open manifest so they stay portable to any compatible viewer or marketplace. Your work is yours, and it leaves with you.

WHAT YOU NEED

A phone running Android 6.0 or later, and a network connection. Camera permission is requested only when you choose the selfie flow, and you can upload a photo from your gallery instead. Deploying an agent on-chain needs a small amount of SOL, typically under 0.01; everything else is free.
