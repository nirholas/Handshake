regionCapacity is a distribution problem, not a supply problem. Unused regional grants are the cheapest capacity there is.

One command audits and rebalances the fleet

Our capacity tool reads every GPU region's grant, holders and headroom, ranks the actions by time to effect, and ports a starved service to a region with free capacity in minutes without a rebuild. It is covered by tests, not tribal knowledge.
