It signs these — it doesn’t spell them

A signer spells names and loanwords and signs everything else. These words have real signs in the avatar’s vocabulary: a handshape, a place on the body, and a movement, on one or both hands. Pick one to watch it. Everything else in a sentence still fingerspells.

Sign to us — we’ll read it

Click start, fingerspell a word at your camera, then stop. Your browser turns the video into hand and body landmarks locally; only those coordinates are sent for recognition. Webcam fingerspelling is imperfect (expect the odd wrong letter), so review the text before you trust it.

Best results: good light, hand in frame, one letter at a time. Numbers and the full A–Z alphabet are supported.

Honest scope: words with a sign are signed, everything else is fingerspelled letter by letter. Signs are strung together in English word order, which is signed English, not ASL grammar — real ASL reorders sentences, inflects verbs through space, and carries grammar on the face.
