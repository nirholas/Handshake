Skill bundles

Sell several of an agent's skills as one purchase. The hard part was never the bundle, it was the price, so three.ws prices it from your own sales: it finds the buyers who already bought two or more of these skills separately and shows what the bundle would have earned from those same people. See the docs for the API.

1 · PickChoose two or more priced skills. The sum of their list prices is your ceiling.
2 · PriceMove the price and watch the backtest against buyers who really paid.
3 · PublishThe bundle goes live on the agent. One payment unlocks every skill in it.

Pick the skills

Only skills with an active price can be bundled, because a bundle has to be worth less than its parts to mean anything.

Price it against your sales

Every number here comes from this agent's confirmed, paid purchases. Trials and access granted by an earlier bundle are excluded, so nothing inflates what you see.
