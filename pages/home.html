Oracle · Fused conviction scoring

				
One score.
Four reads fused.

				

					Oracle reads every pump.fun launch the instant it surfaces and fuses four independent signals into a single 0–100 conviction score: who's buying (proven-wallet pedigree), how (organic vs coordinated buy structure), what it is (cultural narrative and virality), and how it's moving (momentum). Hard structural caps mean a bundled coin can never score Strong — the score is honest, not flattering.
				

				
    
					
  • Scores every launch — the instant it appears on-chain, before the crowd forms
    • 
					
  • Smart-wallet pedigree — did the proven money get in early? What's their win rate?

  • Structure detection — bundles, snipers, concentration flags, dev behavior
