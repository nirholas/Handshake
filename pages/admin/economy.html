If the pulse is quiet

The order matters. Each step rules out a cause that would make every later reading meaningless.

  1. Check the verdict above first. It already applies these rules to live data and names the single most actionable cause.
  2. Is money moving at all? Volume and trades near zero while the activity feed looks busy means the free lanes are carrying the feed and the paid lanes are dead.
  3. Did the paid lanes ever run? Zero attempts (not zero successes) means the governor cut the paid budget because the circulation treasury sat under its reserve.
  4. Read the funding chain. Several roles can share one wallet, so a link can be starved while another role's lower floor reports it healthy. Always compare against the strictest floor.
  5. Separate "dry" from "unreadable". no_spare_usdc genuinely needs funding. usdc_read_failed means an RPC lane broke and the balance is unknown, so sending funds would be treating the wrong problem.
