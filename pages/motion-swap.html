Video motion capture · body swap

Your video. Your avatar. Not your face.

Upload a video of yourself — a house tour, a product demo, a talking walk-through — and get it back with your 3D avatar performing your exact motion instead of you. Pose tracking runs server-side; you stay pixelated under the avatar. Compositing and export happen right here in your browser.

Drop a video of one person

MP4, MOV, or WebM up to 256 MB. The first 90 seconds are processed. Full body in frame tracks best.

Capturing your motion

Tracking your pose frame-by-frame and masking you out. A 30-second clip takes about two minutes.

Avatar

Any rigged humanoid GLB works — one you generated, captured from a selfie, or rigged from a mesh.

How it works

A server worker tracks your body pose in every frame and solves it onto the platform's canonical skeleton — the same retarget engine that drives every avatar animation here. It also segments you out into a mask.

Your browser then plays the original footage, pixelates you under the mask, and pins the avatar over you frame-by-frame, matching your position and size. Export records the composite with the original audio.

The source video is processed transiently and the pose data contains no imagery — the motion clip is just skeleton rotations.
