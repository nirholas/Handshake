      The price is measured, not guessed

      Every quote comes from the mesh itself: the analyzer computes the true solid volume of the repaired model, and the price is that volume in the material you picked. Nothing is estimated from a bounding box.

      USDC on Solana, by decision

      There is no card processor. You pay the exact quoted amount in USDC on Solana, the order is opened against a signed quote, and the price can never move between the quote and the charge.

      Agents can order too

      The same pipeline is exposed to autonomous agents over x402, so a model an agent just generated can be manufactured without a human in the loop. See the API reference.

      Where should it go?

      We keep the minimum a courier needs, and nothing else.
