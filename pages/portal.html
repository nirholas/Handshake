Walk any website.

Paste an address. Portal reads the page's real structure and builds a place out of it: every section becomes a building sized by what it says, every link becomes a door you can step through, every image becomes a billboard. Then walk it, download it as a 3D file, or put it on your own site.

Building

 

  • Asking the site, and its robots.txt, for the page
  • Reading its structure: headings, links, images, code
  • Laying out districts, doors and billboards
  • Rendering the world and bringing your avatar in
