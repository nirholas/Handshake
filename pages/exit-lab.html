Exit Lab

The fleet already spent this SOL. This replays every real closed position through the exact exit code the live worker runs, with the rules changed to whatever you choose, and shows what those same trades would have returned. Nothing here is simulated: every entry, peak and final price is a recorded quote from a position that cost real money and settled on-chain.
