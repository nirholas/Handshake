The alphabet, in the order it is easiest to learn

Closed fists first, open hands next, then the pairs every beginner confuses. A letter turns green once you have held it correctly; your progress is kept on this device. You can also press any letter key to jump straight to it.

This is the practice half of the signing loop. The reference for every letter and number lives at /asl-alphabet, whole words and signed chat replies at /sign-language, and the engine behind all three pages is documented in the sign language docs and shipped as the @three-ws/sign-language package, installable from a clone until its registry publish lands. The avatar, the target diagram, and the grader all read the same handshape spec, so what is taught and what is graded can never drift apart.
