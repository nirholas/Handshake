Score reference

How a sentence becomes a body

Every other way to get a new animation needs data you do not have: a capture stage, a licensed clip library, or a diffusion model trained on one. This needs a sentence, because a human body is a solved kinematics problem once somebody writes down where it should be and when.

A score, not a keyframe dump

The intermediate format is anatomy and timing: posture: "crouch", arms.right.at: "chin", gaze: "aside". No bone names, no quaternions. That is what makes it writable by a language model, readable in a diff, and editable by hand in the box above.

Feet meet the floor, not the hips

Ankles are placed in world space against the ground. The hips are then free to drop, lean and turn, and the legs absorb it through inverse kinematics, which is exactly what a real body does and why a crouch here looks like a crouch.

Balance is measured

The centre of mass is a mass-weighted sum over the segments that carry a body's weight, and the hips move until it sits over whichever feet are actually carrying weight. Lift one foot and the body shifts over the other on its own.

The shoulder helps

An arm is not two bones on a fixed socket. The clavicle swings toward a distant target, worth ten to fifteen centimetres of reach and most of what makes a reach read as a whole body reaching rather than an arm being extended.

Effort is not decoration

Weight, time and flow pick the easing curve, scale the travel, and set how far a limb overshoots before it settles. It is what makes the same three shapes read as a wave, a swat, or a salute.

Deterministic, so it is cacheable

The same score compiles to the same clip, byte for byte, forever. That is what lets a motion be cached at the edge, diffed in review, and pinned in a regression test.

The order each beat is solved in

Reverse any two of these and the result is subtly wrong in a way that is impossible to fix downstream. Solve the arms before the torso and every gesture sits where the chest used to be.

root where the hips are, which everything below hangs from
turn the body's heading, so limbs solve in the turned frame
torso lean, twist and side-bend, shared along the spine
legs ankles placed in world space, so dropping the hips bends the knees
balance the hips nudged until the weight sits over the feet
arms wrists placed against anchors that ride the posed chest
hands shape, after the wrist is where it belongs
gaze last, so a look is relative to the chest it ended up on

Use it in your own code

Refine it by hand

Send any motion to the Animation Studio to keyframe over it, then save it to your account, play it back across the platform, or sell it.

Put it on your site

The clip plays on any humanoid rig through the one-tag embed and the retargeter, which maps Mixamo, Avaturn, VRM, Unreal and Daz bone names onto the same canonical set.
