Why the bar is strict

A creator who generates a coarse mesh chose to, and can regenerate. A visitor browsing the catalog chose nothing: every entry is the platform vouching for itself. One melted blob on the storefront costs more trust than ten rejected generations cost compute.

This is the real gate

The page imports the same module the seeding cron runs, so the rules here cannot drift from the rules in production. Read the full gate documentation for the vision stage that runs after this one.

Structure, not taste

These checks measure whether a model is built well enough to publish: density, weight, texture, bounds, assembly. Whether it looks good is judged separately by a vision model on the catalog path.
