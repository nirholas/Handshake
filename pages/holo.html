Holo Sticker

A holographic foil sticker, rendered live. The die-cut outline, the iridescent foil, the light it reflects, and the peeling corner are all computed in your browser: no images, no model files. Tilt it with your pointer the way you would tilt the real thing, or type your own text and get it cut into a sticker of its own.

How it works

The mark is rasterized, dilated by a disc the way a cutter adds a margin, and traced back into vector outlines with marching squares, holes included. The foil is a physical material with thin-film iridescence, and the peel is a vertex-shader cylinder roll, so the light on the curled lip is real.
