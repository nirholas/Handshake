Never been here? This is the whole thing.

No download, no wallet, no account. It runs in the browser tab you already have open, on a phone or a laptop.

🧍

First, you get a body

Make an avatar from a selfie, pick one from the library, or drag in your own .glb. It animates properly either way: idle, walk and gestures are retargeted onto whatever skeleton you bring.

🌍

Then you drop into the world

A shared 3D town built from the coin itself, with a real day/night clock everyone agrees on. Walk it, drive it, and see everyone else who is in there with you.

💬

You can actually do things

Town chat and voice, emotes and a dance floor, jobs from the board, the general store and bank, King of the Totem at the landmark, and the wheel in the plaza.

🎡

And you can leave with something

A free spin on Fortune's Folly every 12 hours, cosmetics you own, and a commemorative photo of the crowd taken from inside the world.

The run of show

Every time below is your local time, converted in your browser.

The world is at /play and it is open whether or not an event is running. Build an avatar first at /create, read what the in-world economy pays at /play/economy, and see everything that shipped recently on the changelog.
