Payment links

Encode a merchant address, amount, and memo into a shareable link. It renders a full checkout with no backend - the request lives in the URL fragment, which never leaves the browser.

Checksum-validated. A mistyped mixed-case address is rejected before a link is made.
A decimal, or the word dynamic for tips.
Any host that serves pay.html works - self-host it for free on your own Pages.
Fill the form to generate a link.
