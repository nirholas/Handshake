Add Robinhood Chain to your wallet

One click sends your wallet the official EIP-3085 parameters for chain 4663 (RPC, explorer, ETH gas token), derived at runtime from viem's chain definition. Nothing else: no connection request, no signature, no tracking.

Adding the testnet instead? add.html?network=testnet registers chain 46630.
