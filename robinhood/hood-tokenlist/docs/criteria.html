Inclusion criteria

This list is neutral infrastructure. Inclusion is decided by the rules on this page, executed by scripts/refresh.mjs against live chain state, and nothing else. There is no editorial override in either direction. The same rules are unit-tested in tests/criteria.test.mjs.

Canonical assets

1Stock Tokens

A Stock Token is included when, at refresh time, it (a) resolves through Robinhood's shared EIP-1967 beacon 0xe10b6f6B275de231345c20D14Ab812db62151b00, (b) answers symbol(), name(), decimals(), and the ERC-8056 uiMultiplier() on-chain, (c) reconciles with the official contracts registry, and (d) if it has a Chainlink feed, that feed answers latestRoundData() with a positive price. Candidates come from full Blockscout discovery, so the set is the complete on-chain universe, not a curated subset.

2Base assets

USDG (the chain's dollar stablecoin, 6 decimals) and WETH are included from the official contracts registry, identity re-verified on-chain every refresh. Other bridged assets are added only when Robinhood's registry lists them; none currently qualify.

Memecoins

A launchpad token is included only when it passes all of the following at refresh time. Failing any one rule excludes it, and every exclusion is logged with its reason in data/refresh-report.json.

1Provenance

Launched by the NOXA launch factory (TokenLaunched event) or graduated from an Odyssey bonding-curve factory (PoolMigrated event). The full event history of both launchpads is scanned from genesis on every refresh; nothing is added by hand.

2Known quote asset

The token's Uniswap v3 pool must be quoted in WETH or USDG.

3Liquidity of at least $2,500

Total pool value, computed as twice the quote-side reserves, valued live via the on-chain Chainlink ETH/USD and USDG/USD feeds, must be at least $2,500.

4At least 7 days old

Launch (NOXA) or graduation (Odyssey) must be at least 7 days before the refresh.

5At least 50 holders

Blockscout must report 50 or more holders.

6Sellable and transferable

Two live simulations must succeed: a QuoterV2 quote selling one whole token into the quote asset through the token's own pool, and an eth_call of transfer(recipient, 1) from the token's current top holder. A token whose holders cannot sell or move it is a honeypot and is out.

7Schema-valid identity

The on-chain symbol must be 20 characters or fewer with no whitespace; the name must fit the standard's 60-character cap (Stock Token names longer than that drop only the • Robinhood Token suffix). Unreadable identity means exclusion.

8No ticker spoofing

A memecoin whose symbol matches any canonical ticker (a Stock Token, USDG, or WETH, case-insensitive) is excluded outright.

9One token per symbol

When two qualifying memecoins share a symbol, the one with the deeper quote-side liquidity stays; ties break deterministically to the lower address.

Removal

The same rules govern removal: a listed memecoin that no longer passes (liquidity drained, holders collapsed, sell simulation fails) is dropped on the next refresh, and the list's major version increments per the token-lists standard. Stock Tokens are removed only if they stop resolving through the official beacon or vanish from Robinhood's registry.

Refresh cadence

npm run refresh re-verifies every entry live (a full launchpad history rescan, identity multicalls, feed reads, and the memecoin funnel) and rewrites tokenlist.json deterministically: an unchanged list is byte-identical, so a no-op refresh produces no diff. Recommended cadence is weekly, or after notable launchpad activity.

Stock Token eligibility

Stock Tokens are tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd and may not be offered, sold, or delivered to US persons, with additional restrictions in Canada, the UK, and Switzerland. Displaying their data is unrestricted; acquiring them is not available to everyone. Every stock entry carries extensions.eligibility = "not-for-us-persons" so integrators can gate buy flows. This list is data, not an offer of securities.
