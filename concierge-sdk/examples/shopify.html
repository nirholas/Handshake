Small-batch outdoor gear

Larkspur makes jackets, packs, and layers for people who spend more time outside than in. Every piece is made in limited runs, so the assistant in the corner knows the live catalog and can help you find the right thing, compare options, and check shipping.

This is a demo storefront. On a real Shopify store the assistant reads your live catalog automatically. It fetches your store's public /products.json, /collections.json, and policy pages (no API key, no app install, no product feed to maintain), then recommends real products as cards with live prices and links. Here, window.Shopify is faked so you can see auto-detection kick in; the product cards appear once the catalog loads on the actual store.

Install on your Shopify theme

Paste one tag into theme.liquid, just before </body>. Shopping mode turns on by itself because the widget detects the Shopify storefront:

<script type="module"
        src="https://three.ws/concierge/concierge.global.js"
        data-concierge
        data-site-name="Larkspur Supply"
        data-accent="#3f7d5b"
        data-avatar="nova"></script>

That's it. Want to point it at a specific store, force shopping mode, or set the currency? Add data-shop, data-shopping="true", and data-currency.
