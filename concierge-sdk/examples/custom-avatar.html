Bring your own avatar

Instead of the built-in catalog, point customAvatar at any rigged, viseme-capable GLB you host. It should be a humanoid with a skeleton and (ideally) ARKit/Oculus visemes or a jaw morph, so it blinks and lipsyncs. Unrigged meshes are not supported, they would render as a frozen statue.

This example uses one of the three.ws catalog GLBs by absolute URL to stand in for your own file; replace the URL with yours.
