Reporting something

You do not need a lawyer to reach us, and you do not need to file a formal notice before we will look at a problem. Tell us the URL and what is wrong with it.

  • dmca@three.ws Copyright. Your work is being reproduced without permission, or you want your publication removed from a feed we aggregate.
  • abuse@three.ws Content that breaks the Content Policy: impersonation, harassment, fraud, or illegal material.
  • privacy@three.ws Data requests: access, correction, deletion, or a question about how your data is handled.
  • legal@three.ws Anything else, including questions about these policies.
  • support@three.ws Product problems that are not a legal matter.

Publishers

three.ws aggregates crypto news headlines and adds its own analysis. We quote a short lead excerpt and link to you for the rest; we do not host your article body. If you believe we are showing more than that, or you want your publication dropped from the aggregator entirely, email dmca@three.ws and we will act on it. The full policy, including the limits we enforce in code, is documented at Publisher rights in the news reader.
