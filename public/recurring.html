Recurring payments

Money that moves on a schedule, with every charge on the record

A recurring payment pulls a fixed amount of USDC from your wallet on a period you choose, inside a permission you signed once. A DCA schedule does the same and swaps the result into a token. Both run on the hourly crons, both can be paused and resumed without re-signing, and both keep a per-charge ledger you can read here.
