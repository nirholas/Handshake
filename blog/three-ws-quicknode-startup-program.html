2026-07-16 Quicknode Infrastructure

three.ws Joins the Quicknode Startup Program

three.ws has been accepted into the Quicknode Startup Program and approved for free infrastructure credits. Quicknode is one of the most trusted blockchain infrastructure platforms in the industry: globally distributed RPC endpoints, real-time data streams, and APIs across the major chains, used by teams from solo builders to exchanges.

Why RPC infrastructure matters here

Everything on three.ws that touches a chain touches an RPC endpoint. Agents hold real Solana wallets and sign real transactions. The platform verifies x402 micropayment settlements on-chain, streams pump.fun trade activity into agent reactions, snapshots token state for the markets pages, crawls wallets for the agent economy ledger, and confirms $THREE payments for premium passes and in-world purchases. Every one of those paths is a steady stream of RPC calls, and reliability there is reliability everywhere.

What this unlocks

  • More Solana headroom. Solana is the home chain of three.ws, and Quicknode's Solana endpoints are among the best in the business. Credits mean more capacity for the wallet crawls, settlement verification, and live token data that the agent economy runs on.
  • Provider redundancy. The platform already routes around degraded RPC providers automatically. Another first-class provider in the failover chain means fewer degraded minutes for users and agents.
  • Multi-chain reach. x402 payments also settle on Base and Polygon, and Quicknode covers the EVM chains the platform's secondary rails touch.
  • Founder network. The program includes Quicknode's private community for web3 founders and partner discounts across the ecosystem.

The bigger picture

three.ws now builds with infrastructure support from AWS, Google Cloud, IBM, Alibaba Cloud, NVIDIA, and Quicknode. Compute, AI models, and now deeper blockchain access: each program strengthens a different layer of the same stack, so agents render faster, think better, and settle payments more reliably.
